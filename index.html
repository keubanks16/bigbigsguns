Auto-backup is on

Every time you add, edit or delete a gun, it's encrypted on this phone and saved to your own private GitHub repo. On a new phone, enter the same details here and your whole catalog comes back. Setup steps

Write the password down somewhere safe. It can't be recovered, and without it the backup can't be opened — by anyone, including you.

Smart photo recognition

Without a key, the app reads text off your photos on-device (good for serial numbers). Add an Anthropic API key and it can also recognize the make and model from the gun itself. The key stays on this device only.

Backup file (optional extra copy)

Backups are locked with a password before they leave this phone, so you can safely keep them in Google Drive, iCloud, OneDrive or email — nobody can open them without your password. Write the password down somewhere safe; it can't be recovered.
